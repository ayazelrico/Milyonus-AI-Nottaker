# Third-party notices

## Bundled fonts

The font files in app/Sources/DesignSystem/Resources/Fonts are unmodified redistributable assets
from the Google Fonts repository, pinned to commit 9710da1eacb3be272583c3224dcb70f9da6eadbb.

- Inter Tight variable upright: ofl/intertight/InterTight[wght].ttf, stored as InterTight.ttf.
- Instrument Serif italic: ofl/instrumentserif/InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf.
- Both font families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full licenses and copyright notices are
  included beside the fonts as InterTight-OFL.txt and InstrumentSerif-OFL.txt.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/9710da1eacb3be272583c3224dcb70f9da6eadbb/ofl

Swift and TypeScript development dependencies retain their own upstream licenses. The dependency
lockfile records backend versions. Supplied Milyonus brand images belong to their respective owner.
